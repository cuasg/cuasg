# Allen Giammarrusco

### Systems Builder | Automation · AI · Business Process Engineering

I build practical systems that turn inefficient business processes into reliable, repeatable workflows.

My background sits at the intersection of **business operations, sales, process design, and technology**. I tend to work from the problem outward: understand how the process actually works, identify the friction, design a better approach, then build and deploy the system that supports it.

I am especially interested in work where I can move through the full lifecycle:

**Requirements → System Design → Build → Test → Deploy → Improve → Handoff**

Rather than specializing in one language or framework, I use the tools that fit the problem. My projects span cloud automation, internal business applications, AI-assisted workflows, data systems, IoT, self-hosted infrastructure, and hardware/software integration.

AI is part of my engineering workflow. I use it as a force multiplier for implementation, research, debugging, testing, and iteration while keeping the architecture, requirements, business logic, validation, and final decisions grounded in the problem I am solving.

## How I Work

The common thread across these projects is not a particular technology. It is **systems thinking**.

I enjoy taking ambiguous operational problems and turning them into something concrete: requirements, workflows, architecture, interfaces, automation, tests, and ultimately a working system.

That often means bridging groups that do not naturally speak the same language: business users, operations teams, customers, software, data, infrastructure, and AI.

I am most energized by project-based work where there is a real problem to understand, something useful to build, and a clear point where the finished system can be put into people's hands.
